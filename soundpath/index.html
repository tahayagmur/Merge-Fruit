The problem

A scan plan is still drawn line by line, weld by weld.

01

Drawn by hand or in CAD

Angles, skips and index offsets are redrawn for every new thickness, bevel or probe.

02

Gaps show up late

A missed root or fusion face is often found at the calibration block or on site, not on paper.

03

Records in many places

Technique sheet, block drawing, focal laws and approval live in separate files.

What SoundPath does

Set up the weld and the probe. See the result as you change them.

Beam paths on plate and pipe

  • Phased array, conventional and TOFD on butt welds, girth welds and long seams.
  • Coverage map of the weld and heat-affected zone, leg by leg.
  • Drag the wedge to move the probe; hover the part for sound path and time of flight.

Calibration before the block

  • Estimated TCG for each side-drilled hole, with attenuation and beam spreading shown separately.
  • Calibration block drawing sized from the technique, saved as SVG or DXF for CAD.
  • Focal laws exported for the instrument, and laws read back from the instrument checked against the plan.

Checks

A pass / warn / fail list as you work, for example focus beyond the near field.

Resolution map

Where in the weld the beam is focused and where it is not.

Compare

Two set-ups side by side before you choose one.

Approved techniques

Equipment
